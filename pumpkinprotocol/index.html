The cozy protocol

Bitcoin, but make it feel like coming home.

Pumpkin Protocol deliberately trades chrome, neon and trading-floor energy for clay, carved wood, lantern light and characters you actually want to spend time with.

The visual language is soft and dimensional, but never glossy: terracotta, sage, amber, cranberry, canvas cream and charcoal ink, with hand-cut texture sitting over rounded clay forms.

“A tiny harvest world with enough warmth to make checking your sats feel like tending a garden.”
