Curator's Statement

"Most modern software shouts for your attention like a Times Square billboard. We preferred to hang a single framed photograph of a nocturnal trash bandit on your glass and let seventeen syllables do the talking."

Every plate in Raccoon-Haiku-a-Day was personally photographed, composed, and hand-set by Bryan Hansen (@raccoonhaiku). At midnight, your Home Screen quietly turns the page — or tap any plate in the archive to exhibit it for the evening.

Home Screen & iPhone Duo Widget Installation

Raccoon-Haiku-a-Day is designed for the Large Square Home Screen canvas on iPhone and iPhone Duo:

  1. Press and hold on your Home Screen where you would like your daily Raccoon Haiku plate exhibited.
  2. Tap Edit in the top corner, then select Add Widget.
  3. Search for Raccoon-Haiku-a-Day (or HaiCoon).
  4. Select the Large Square widget and tap Add Widget.
  5. Open the gallery app anytime and tap any of the 61 archival plates to exhibit that print on your Home Screen for the rest of the evening. At midnight, the widget automatically advances to the next daily plate.

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 9, 2026

Raccoon-Haiku-a-Day ("HaiCoon-a-Day", published by Bee Four Inc.) is built as a quiet, private art exhibition on your device. We respect your privacy completely and collect zero personal data.

  • No Account Required & No Personal Data Collected: The app does not require registration, sign-in, or personal identifiers. All 61 original Raccoon Haiku photographic plates and poems are bundled locally inside the application.
  • On-Device Widget Storage Only: When you tap a plate in the gallery to feature it on your Home Screen widget, your selection and install date are stored strictly locally on your device using an Apple App Group container (group.com.beefour.raccoonhaiku) so the widget extension can display your chosen print. This data never leaves your device.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ad Trackers: Raccoon-Haiku-a-Day contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and no behavioral analytics scripts.
  • StoreKit In-App Subscriptions: Optional Weekly Gallery Patronage subscriptions are processed directly and securely by Apple via StoreKit 2. Bee Four Inc. never receives or stores your payment card or billing details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Apple Account Settings.
  • Children's Privacy: Our exhibition is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone of any age.
  • Contact Us: Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to beefourhansen@gmail.com.
