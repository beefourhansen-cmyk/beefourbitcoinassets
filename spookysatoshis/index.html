No account. No email. No wallet keys.

The short version

Spooky Satoshis does not ask you to create an account, and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any wallet key. Your game progress is stored on your own device. We show ads, and the ad SDKs collect an advertising identifier so we can keep the game free.

Information stored on your device

Your progress — including your sat balance, streak, haunters, relics and transaction history — is stored locally on your device in the app's private storage. It is not uploaded to us. Deleting the app deletes it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Advertising

We show advertising supplied by Google AdMob, including rewarded video and 300×250 banner ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android AD_ID);
  • device and app information, coarse location derived from IP, and ad interaction data;
  • diagnostic data.

We use this to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and keep the game free. Google's handling of that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change or withdraw that choice at any time from the privacy-options entry point in the app.

On iOS we ask for tracking permission before accessing the advertising identifier; declining does not prevent you from playing.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your card details. Google provides us only with the product ID and a purchase token so we can grant what you bought.

Sats, relics and rewards — important

Sats are in-app reward points. They:

  • are not bitcoin and are not backed by bitcoin or any other asset;
  • cannot be transferred, traded, sold, or exchanged;
  • have no cash value;
  • are not currently redeemable — payout settlement is closed.

Nothing in the game is a financial product, an investment, or a promise of income. Purchases grant in-game capacity only.

Children

Spooky Satoshis is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will act on it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers described above (Google AdMob for advertising, Google Play for purchases) and where the law requires it.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of personalised ads via the in-app privacy options.
  • Delete your data by uninstalling the app.
  • Request information using the contact address below.

Retention

On-device game data persists until you uninstall or clear app storage. Advertising and purchase data are retained by Google according to their policies.
