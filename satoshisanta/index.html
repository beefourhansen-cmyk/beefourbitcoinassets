Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Effective October 22, 2026 · Bee Four Inc. (Vancouver, BC, Canada) · Contact: support@beefourbitcoin.com · Authorized Seller File (app-ads.txt)

1. Local-First Progress (No Account or Personal Data Required)

Satoshi Santa does not require an account and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or wallet keys. Your sleigh progress, streak, reindeer timers, and Snow Globe ornaments are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all local app data.

2. Advertising, Mediation & Consent (GDPR / CCPA / UMP)

To keep Satoshi Santa free, we show voluntary Rewarded Videos and 300×250 MREC banners supplied by Google AdMob and authorized mediation partners (Unity Ads, Meta Audience Network, Pangle). These SDKs may collect your device Advertising ID (Android AD_ID), coarse IP-based location, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Where required by law (EEA/UK/US states), we request consent via Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), and you can update your choices anytime via the in-app Privacy Options button.

3. In-App Reward Units (“Sats”) — No Cash Value

All “sats” / “Satoshis” and ornaments in Satoshi Santa are virtual in-app gameplay items only. They are not cryptocurrency, are not backed by Bitcoin or any financial asset, carry no cash value, cannot be transferred or exchanged, and payout settlement is closed. Nothing in the app constitutes a financial product or investment.

4. Google Play Billing, Children’s Privacy & Contact

Optional Gift Shop purchases are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing; Bee Four Inc. never receives or stores your payment card details. Satoshi Santa is not directed to children under 13. For data inquiries or support, email support@beefourbitcoin.com.
